Meet your teacher

A little about Mandi

🎓 Mandi is a qualified music teacher with experience teaching children and adults of all ages. She believes that music is one of the best ways to build confidence and self-esteem, express emotions creatively, learn fine motor skills, and develop a strong musical ear.

👶 Mandi loves working with under 5s, using a gentle, encouraging approach that focuses on play and sound exploration. She’s also happy to support children and adults through ABRSM piano exams, always adapting to each learner’s personality, pace, and interests.

🎹 With years of experience playing piano and singing, Mandi has achieved her Grade 8 in Rock School vocals, a distinction in music theory, and a first-class degree in Music Technology. She also loves to compose, mix, and master her own music.

💖 At the heart of it all is Mandi’s deep love of teaching — and a genuine delight in watching learners grow through music. Her goal isn’t just to teach notes and songs, but to nurture curiosity, confidence, and a lifelong love of making music.
